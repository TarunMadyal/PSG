# Thermal Printer — Buying & Setup Guide

PSG Padmashree Garments prints bills on a **Bluetooth thermal printer** using the
standard **ESC/POS** protocol. Every sale is saved *before* printing, so a
printer problem never loses a bill — you can always reprint from the receipt
screen.

---

## 1. Which printer to buy

Look for these three things on the listing:

- **Thermal receipt printer** (no ink — uses thermal paper rolls)
- **Bluetooth** support (the app connects over Bluetooth)
- **ESC/POS** compatible (almost all of them are; the listing usually says so)

### Recommended: 80 mm Bluetooth printer (for a fixed counter)
Bigger paper, roomier itemised bills, faster. Best for a shop counter.

- Search Amazon.in: **https://www.amazon.in/s?k=80mm+bluetooth+thermal+printer+esc+pos**
- Reliable models commonly available in India: **Black Copper BC‑85AC (80mm BT)**,
  **Everycom EC‑805 (80mm BT)**, **Rugtek / Pegmatite 80mm Bluetooth**.
- Paper: **80 mm thermal rolls** (easy to buy in bulk).

### Budget / portable: 58 mm Bluetooth printer
Smaller, handheld, cheaper. Good if you also want to bill while moving around.

- Search Amazon.in: **https://www.amazon.in/s?k=58mm+bluetooth+thermal+printer**
- Common models: **Everycom EC‑58**, **Atpos AT‑302**, any "58mm Bluetooth POS printer".
- Paper: **58 mm thermal rolls**.

> Tip: buy a brand with reviews mentioning "ESC/POS" and "Bluetooth". Add a small
> **UPS / power bank** for the counter so a power cut never stops billing.

After buying, set the matching **Receipt width (58 mm or 80 mm)** in the app
under **Settings → Receipt width**.

---

## 2. One-time setup

1. **Load the paper roll** and switch the printer on.
2. On your Android tablet/phone: open **Settings → Bluetooth**, tap **Pair new
   device**, and pair the printer (PIN is usually `0000` or `1234` if asked).
3. Open the **PSG app → Settings → Printer → Select printer**.
4. Allow the Bluetooth permission when the app asks.
5. Pick your printer from the list and tap it. It's now saved as the default.
6. Tap **Test print** — a short test page should come out.

That's it. The printer stays saved; the app reconnects automatically.

---

## 3. Printing a bill

1. Add items on the **Billing** screen and tap **Charge**.
2. On the confirmation, tap **Print**.
3. The bill prints with your **shop name, address, items, total, and the
   "thank you" footer** you set in Settings.

Need another copy? Re-open the sale and print again — nothing is lost.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "No printer set up yet" | Settings → Printer → Select printer |
| "Bluetooth is off" | Turn on Bluetooth in the device settings |
| "Could not connect" | Make sure the printer is **on**, charged, and in range; print again |
| Nothing prints / blank | Check the paper roll is loaded the right way up |
| Garbled text | Make sure **Receipt width** in Settings matches your paper (58 vs 80 mm) |
| Printer not in the list | Pair it in the device's Bluetooth settings first, then tap **refresh** |
